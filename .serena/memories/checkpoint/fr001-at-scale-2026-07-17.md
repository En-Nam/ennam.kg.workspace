### VERDICT: MARGINAL

`kg_graph_retrieve`'s 1-hop `similar_to` expansion — keep it, **do not build
FR-002/003/005 on it as a retrieval floor.** The mechanism is real (fires
constantly at 77-doc scale) but rarely delivers a genuine novel answer.

### Three scale metrics vs Dasin baseline

| Metric | Dasin (N=10, 9 docs) | Cảng Định An (N=21, 77 docs) |
|---|---|---|
| `hop1_only_rate` | 0.40 (4/10 queries) | **1.0 (21/21 queries)** — rose sharply |
| `answer_rate` | 0.25 (1/4 hop1-only queries, confound-verified) | **≈0.048 (1/21 queries, confound-verified; 88/175 hop1-only rows sampled, not exhaustive)** — fell sharply despite firing far more often |

| `total_hop1_only_docs` | 8 | 132 |
| `total_true_cross_doc_hop1` | 25 | 219 |

Decision logic (brief's exact criteria): `hop1_only_rate` holding/rising
rules out NOT LOAD-BEARING (mechanism didn't collapse — intensified).
`answer_rate` not materially >0 (lower than Dasin's already-thin 25%) rules
out LOAD-BEARING. What's left: MARGINAL — "mechanism fires but rarely
answers."

### The one confirmed instance (only surviving Yes across 88 judged snippets)

Query `h01` ("tài sản cố định trên bảng cân đối kế toán"), doc `2a5a59e7`,
chunk `755431dc` — OCR-garbled but genuine balance-sheet fixed-asset line
items ("Tài sản cổ định thu tài chính", "Bất động sản đầu tư" w/ figures).
Confound check: queried `kg_search_chunks` with the chunk's own distinctive
numeric phrasing (`"Tài sản cổ định thu tài chính Bất động sản đầu tư
167.632.204.917"`, limit 10) → `total_count: 10`, target doc **does NOT
appear** in the 10 results. Plain search genuinely cannot reach this chunk;
the `similar_to` edge can. Same underlying mechanism as Dasin's decisive X4
instance (chunk whose aggregate embedding is about something else, reached
via a chunk→chunk edge from a topically-relevant seed) — confirms the
mechanism generalizes, just rarely fires productively at this scale.
